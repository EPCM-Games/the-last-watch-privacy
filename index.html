The Last Watch – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026. Applies to the game on iPhone, iPad, Android, Windows and the web.

The short version

The game has no accounts of its own and never asks for your email. Your progress stays on your device, and on phones a copy is also kept in your Google Play Games or Game Center account when you're signed in to it. If you pick a leaderboard name, that name and your scores are public. The mobile app shows optional ads through Google AdMob, which uses a device identifier for advertising. You can delete your leaderboard data in the game at any time.

Who we are

The Last Watch is made by EagleShark Studios LLC (also known as EPCM Games), 6701 Corporate Dr, Ste N, Johnston, IA 50131, United States. We are the controller of the information described here. Contact: lastwatchgame@gmail.com.

Your age

The first time the game opens it asks what year you were born. The year is not saved and not sent to us: the game only remembers, on your device, which age group you are in. If you are under 13, the game shows no ads, sends no play stats and doesn't let you pick a leaderboard name. If you are 13 to 17, ads are never personalised and play stats stay off.

On your device

Your progress, settings and towers are saved on your device. On Android and iPhone, when you're signed in to Google Play Games or Game Center, the game also keeps a copy of your progress in that account (on Google's servers, or in your iCloud) so it comes back on a new phone; Google and Apple hold it under their own privacy policies. We can't see it. Deleting the app deletes the copy on your device, unless it was saved to that account or you copied a save code first (Settings → Save backup). Reminders the game shows are scheduled on your device and send us nothing.

Leaderboards

Joining is optional. If you pick a name, our leaderboard server (Supabase, in the United States) stores that name, a random key your device makes so it can prove it's you (we keep only a scrambled copy), your scores and runs, runs you choose to publish, the towers you've collected, your wins and the look you chose for your name. Your name, scores, published runs and towers are public to every player, and the top of each board is also posted in our Discord server. We don't store your email, your real name or your location.

You can delete all of it at any time in Settings → Leaderboard data → Delete. Names and published runs that break the rules can be reported; a person on our team reviews reports and can rename or remove them. No longer have the game? Email lastwatchgame@gmail.com with your leaderboard name and we'll delete it and your scores.

Error reports

If the game hits a programming error, it sends a short report to the same server so we can fix it: the error message, where in the game's code it happened, the game version, the platform and the phone's model and system version (for example "Android 14 Pixel 7"). It doesn't include your leaderboard name or your key. Reports are deleted after 60 days.

Play stats

To see how many people play and where new players get stuck, the game can send a few moments to the same server: when the app opens, when the first-run walkthrough ends, when a run ends (the mode, whether you won, how long it lasted and the round), when you open the monthly pass or Support screens, and when you share a run. Each comes with a random number this copy of the game made for itself, the game version and the platform. It isn't your leaderboard key and isn't linked to your name, your purchases or your advertising identifier. We delete these after 90 days.

In Europe play stats are off unless you switch them on when the game first opens; elsewhere they are on unless you switch them off. Change it any time in Settings → Share play stats.

Monthly pass

So your monthly pass progress comes back on a new phone, the game keeps one number per season on the same server: the tier you've reached, filed under a random key this copy of the game made (we store only a scrambled copy). The key travels with your cloud save and save codes. It isn't linked to your name.

Ads

The Android and iPhone versions have optional rewarded ads from Google AdMob: they only play when you choose to watch one. The Windows and web versions have no ads. When an ad loads, Google may collect your device's advertising identifier, your IP address and the rough location it suggests, device and app information and how you interact with the ad, to show and measure ads and prevent fraud.

In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland you're asked for consent before any ad loads, and can change your choice in Settings → Ad privacy. On iPhone you're also asked whether apps may track you; saying no still allows ads, just not personalised ones. Google's own policy: policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

Purchases

The Supporter Package and each season's premium monthly pass are one-time purchases (not subscriptions) made through the App Store or Google Play. They handle the payment; we never see your card or account details. Restore purchases brings them back on a new device.

To show the gold supporter name, our leaderboard server asks Google Play or the App Store whether the Supporter Package was bought, and keeps the store's reference for that purchase (a purchase token or transaction number and the order number, not your store account) with your leaderboard name, so one purchase can't light every name. It is deleted when you delete your leaderboard data. Nothing is stored on our server about monthly pass purchases.

Network information

Like any online service, our server sees the IP address of a device that contacts it. We use it only for a short time to stop abuse (for example, too many requests from one connection) and don't keep it with your name or scores.

Who we share with

We don't sell your information and don't share it for cross-context behavioural advertising ourselves. It is handled only by the services named here: Supabase (the leaderboard server), Google (ads, Google Play purchases and Play Games), Apple (App Store purchases and Game Center), Discord (the public leaderboard posts) and GitHub (this website), or when the law requires it. The game links to our Discord server; what you post there is covered by Discord's own policy.

How long we keep it

Leaderboard names, scores and profiles: until you delete them or the leaderboards close. Error reports: 60 days. Play stats: 90 days. Monthly pass tiers: while the pass runs; email us a save code and we'll delete them sooner. Supporter purchase references: until you delete your leaderboard data.

Your rights

Wherever you live, you can ask us what we hold about you, and ask us to correct or delete it: email lastwatchgame@gmail.com and we'll answer within one month.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, we rely on: your consent for ads and for play stats (withdraw it any time in Settings); our legitimate interest in running the leaderboard you chose to join, checking supporter purchases, fixing errors, restoring your monthly pass and stopping abuse; and our contract with you for purchases. You also have the right to restrict or object to our use of your information, to receive a copy of it, and to complain to your data protection authority (in the UK, the Information Commissioner's Office).

Our service providers process information in the United States and other countries. Where the law requires it, those transfers rely on safeguards such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

The game makes no automated decisions about you that have legal or similar effects. It does not respond to browser "Do Not Track" signals, because it doesn't track you across other apps or websites; ad choices are made in the consent screens described above.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their personal information. Players who tell the game they are under 13 get no ads, no play stats and no leaderboard name. If you believe a child has given us a leaderboard name, email us and we'll delete it.

Security

Information is sent over encrypted (HTTPS) connections, and the leaderboard database can only be reached through the game's own limited requests.

Contact and changes

Questions or requests about your data: EagleShark Studios LLC, lastwatchgame@gmail.com. If this policy changes, the new version appears on this page and in the game under Settings → Privacy, with a new date.
