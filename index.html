The Last Watch – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 8, 2026

This policy explains what information The Last Watch ("the game"), made by EPCM Games, collects and how it is used. It covers the game on Android, iPhone and iPad, Windows and the web. If you have any questions, email lastwatchgame@gmail.com.

No account needed

You can play without creating an account or giving us your name, email address, phone number, contacts or location. Your progress (unlocked towers, achievements, settings) is saved on your own device.

Online leaderboard (optional)

If you choose a leaderboard name, the game sends the following to our leaderboard server so your results can appear on the daily and all-time boards:

  • the display name you choose (shown publicly to other players);
  • your game results, such as score, time survived, whether you won and the tower skin you used;
  • a random code created on your device that lets the server recognise your later results. It is not linked to your identity, and only a scrambled (hashed) copy is stored.

The leaderboard is hosted by Supabase. We use this information only to run the leaderboards. Display names are checked against a list of blocked words, and we may hide names that are offensive.

If you never choose a name, nothing is sent to the leaderboard.

Error reports

If the game hits a programming error, it sends a short report to the same server as the leaderboard so we can fix it: the error message, where in the game's code it happened, the game version, the platform and the device's model and system version (for example "Android 14 Pixel 7"). It never includes your leaderboard name, your code or anything else that identifies you. Old reports are deleted after 60 days.

Play stats (you can turn these off)

To see how many people play and where new players get stuck, the game sends a few anonymous moments to the same server: when the app opens, when the first-run walkthrough ends (finished or skipped), when a run ends (the mode, whether you won, how long it lasted and the round reached), when you open the battle pass or Support screens, and when you share a run. Each comes with a random number this copy of the game made for itself, the game version and the platform. It isn't your leaderboard code and isn't linked to your name, your purchases or your advertising ID. We delete these after 90 days. You can turn them off at any time in Settings → Share play stats.

Battle pass

So your battle pass comes back on a new device, the game keeps one number per season on the same server: the tier you have reached, filed under a random code this copy of the game made (only a scrambled copy of the code is stored). The code travels with your cloud save and save codes. It isn't linked to your name, and nothing about what you bought is stored there.

Optional rewarded ads

The Android and iPhone/iPad versions offer optional ads that you can choose to watch for a reward. Ads never play unless you choose to watch one. Watching them helps support us, and we hope to keep the game free of forced ads. These ads are provided by Google AdMob. When an ad is shown, Google may collect information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location derived from it, device and app information, and how you interact with the ad, in order to show and measure ads and prevent fraud. If you are in the European Economic Area or the UK, the game asks for your consent before loading ads. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Ad privacy. On iPhone and iPad, the game also asks for permission (Apple's App Tracking Transparency) before your device's advertising identifier can be used for personalised ads; if you say no, any ad you choose to watch is not personalised. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device's settings (on iPhone and iPad, under Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking). The Windows and web versions show no ads. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

In-app purchase

The optional Supporter Pack and each season's premium battle pass are one-time purchases (not subscriptions) sold through Google Play on Android and the Apple App Store on iPhone and iPad. Payment is handled entirely by Google or Apple; we never see your payment details. The game only receives confirmation that the purchase was made so it can unlock the reward, and we don't store purchases on our server: Restore purchases asks Google or Apple again on a new device.

Sharing

We do not sell your information. Apart from the service providers named above (Supabase for the leaderboard, Google for ads and Android purchases, Apple for App Store purchases), we do not share it with anyone, unless required by law.

Keeping and deleting your data

Leaderboard entries are kept while the leaderboards run. To have your leaderboard name and results deleted, email lastwatchgame@gmail.com with your display name and we will remove them. Error reports are deleted after 60 days and play stats after 90 days. Battle pass tiers are kept so they can come back on a new device; to have them deleted, email us a save code from the game (Settings → Copy save code) and we will remove them. Uninstalling the game deletes the progress stored on your device.

Your rights (EEA and UK)

We use your information on these legal bases: your consent for ads (players in the European Economic Area and the UK are asked before any ad loads, and can change their choice at any time in Settings → Ad privacy); our legitimate interest in running the leaderboard you chose to join, fixing errors, keeping your battle pass and understanding how the game is played (you can turn play stats off in Settings); and the performance of a contract for in-app purchases.

You have the right to access, correct or delete your information, to restrict or object to its use, to receive a copy of it, and to withdraw your consent at any time. To use any of these rights, email lastwatchgame@gmail.com and we will respond within one month. You can also complain to your local data protection authority (in the UK, the Information Commissioner's Office).

Our service providers may process your information outside your country, including in the United States. Where they do, they rely on safeguards such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

The game does not make automated decisions about you that have legal or similarly significant effects.

Children

The game is intended for players aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has submitted a leaderboard name, contact us and we will delete it.

Security

Information sent to the leaderboard is transmitted over encrypted (HTTPS) connections.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
