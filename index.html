The Last Watch – Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 14, 2026

This policy explains what information The Last Watch ("the game"), made by EPCM Games, collects and how it is used. If you have any questions, email lastwatchgame@gmail.com.

No account needed

You can play without creating an account or giving us your name, email address, phone number, contacts or location. Your progress (unlocked towers, achievements, settings) is saved on your own device.

Online leaderboard (optional)

If you choose a leaderboard name, the game sends the following to our leaderboard server so your results can appear on the daily and all-time boards:

  • the display name you choose (shown publicly to other players);
  • your game results, such as score, time survived, whether you won and the tower skin you used;
  • a random code created on your device that lets the server recognise your later results. It is not linked to your identity, and only a scrambled (hashed) copy is stored.

The leaderboard is hosted by Supabase. We use this information only to run the leaderboards. Display names are checked against a list of blocked words, and we may hide names that are offensive.

If you never choose a name, nothing is sent to the leaderboard.

Optional rewarded ads

The Android version offers optional ads that you can choose to watch for a reward. Ads never play unless you choose to watch one. Watching them helps support us, and we hope to keep the game free of forced ads. These ads are provided by Google AdMob. When an ad is shown, Google may collect information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location derived from it, device and app information, and how you interact with the ad, in order to show and measure ads and prevent fraud. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device's settings. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

In-app purchase

The optional Supporter Pack is sold through Google Play. Payment is handled entirely by Google; we never see your payment details. The game only receives confirmation that the purchase was made so it can unlock the reward.

Sharing

We do not sell your information. Apart from the service providers named above (Supabase for the leaderboard, Google for ads and purchases), we do not share it with anyone, unless required by law.

Keeping and deleting your data

Leaderboard entries are kept while the leaderboards run. To have your leaderboard name and results deleted, email lastwatchgame@gmail.com with your display name and we will remove them. Uninstalling the game deletes the progress stored on your device.

Children

The game is intended for players aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has submitted a leaderboard name, contact us and we will delete it.

Security

Information sent to the leaderboard is transmitted over encrypted (HTTPS) connections.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
